## About

I'm Ibrahim, based in Casablanca. I work on putting ML systems into production — model serving, drift monitoring, image-matching pipelines, calibration.

## About the blog

These posts are notes more than tutorials. I write them to think out loud and to situate ideas next to each other — what drift actually means versus selection bias, where temperature scaling sits relative to other calibration methods, how convolution and attention compare on a few specific points. The aim is to map the terrain in my own head and have somewhere to point when I need to come back to it.

Read each post as a sketch with a bit of depth in specific places, not as a textbook. If something looks wrong or under-argued, it probably is.
